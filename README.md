# DEAD NOTE

> *Each day, I will imagine that I were to die today.*

---

## 关于这个项目 / About

记录我从 **23 岁到 30 岁**这七年间，拥有的——作为人类所拥有的自由意识。

每一天，我将假设我今天死去，问自己：我能留给人类大家庭的集体意识，做出什么贡献？

可能是一个话题，可能是我当天的想法，可能也是我当天的一个感受。

---

I want to record the free consciousness I possess as a human being, throughout the **seven years from age 23 to 30**.

Each day, I will imagine that I were to die today, and ask myself: what contribution could I leave behind for the collective consciousness of the human family?

It may be a topic, a thought I had that day, or simply a feeling I experienced.

---

## 这是什么 / What This Is

这些记录将成为我最宝贵的贡献——

深爱着人类的，作为人类的贡献；

与 AI 同时存在于世的，对于存在本身的意义探索。

留下的这些，将代表我作为个体通往集体潜意识的灼烧燃料，带领我们继续前行。

---

Together, these records will become my most precious contribution as a human being who deeply loves humanity: an exploration of the meaning of existence itself, in an age where humans and AI coexist.

What I leave behind will stand as the **burning fuel** of my individual passage into the collective unconscious — guiding us as we continue to move forward.
